Inspiration

Inspired by Dreamland Margate, an unapologetically fun seaside venue at the heart of the town’s cultural revival — and the other Dreamland, brought to life through Lewis Carroll’s playfully subversive imagination. A belief in creativity’s power to transform places and open up new worlds.
